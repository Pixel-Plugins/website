Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your data.

Last updated: August 24, 2026.

PixelPlugins LLC ("PixelPlugins," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through pixelplugins.com and our business relationship, how we use it, the services that help us operate this website, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

  • Information you provide: your name, work email, company, project details, support communications, and other information you choose to send us.
  • Business and transaction information: project, account, subscription, license, billing, and payment-related records. Payment card details are handled by our payment providers rather than stored directly by us.
  • Website usage information, with consent: pages viewed, approximate location, browser and device characteristics, referral source, and interactions such as selected calls to action or submitted inquiry forms. We do not intentionally send the contents of contact-form fields to analytics providers.
  • Technical and security information: information needed to deliver the website, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors, and protect our services.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide, operate, and maintain our services
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions, licenses, or project-related services
  • To communicate with you and provide customer support
  • To plan, deliver, maintain, and improve projects, products, and services
  • To understand and improve our website when you consent to analytics
  • To protect our website, clients, users, and business from fraud, spam, misuse, or security threats

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data based on one or more of the following legal grounds: your consent, the need to perform a contract, compliance with legal obligations, or our legitimate business interests.

4. Service Providers and Data Sharing

  • We do not sell or rent your personal data.
  • Formspree processes website inquiry forms on our behalf.
  • Google reCAPTCHA helps protect forms from spam and abuse.
  • Google Analytics 4 helps us understand website use only after analytics consent is granted.
  • Microsoft Clarity helps us understand navigation and interaction patterns only after analytics consent is granted. Form inputs are excluded from the event data we intentionally send, and Clarity applies its own input masking controls.
  • We may also use hosting, payment, communication, and professional service providers as needed to operate our business and deliver services.
  • We may disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or safety, or as part of a business transaction subject to appropriate safeguards.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected, including providing services, maintaining business records, complying with legal obligations, resolving disputes, and enforcing agreements. Retention periods vary by data type and business need.

6. Analytics Consent, Cookies, and Local Storage

Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity do not load on this website unless you select Accept analytics. If you reject analytics or have not made a choice, we do not load those analytics scripts. Advertising storage, ad user data, and ad personalization remain disabled because this implementation is for website analytics, not personalized advertising.

We store your analytics choice in your browser's local storage so the site can remember it. This preference record is necessary to honor your choice and includes the choice, consent-version number, and update time. Essential technologies used for security and form delivery may operate independently of your analytics choice.

You can accept, reject, or change your choice at any time. If you revoke consent, we send updated denied consent signals, disable further Google Analytics events on the page, ask Clarity to revoke consent, and attempt to remove associated first-party analytics cookies. Use to review your choice.

7. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed or stored in countries outside your own, depending on the location of our service providers and infrastructure.

9. Your Rights and Choices

  • You may request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • You may request correction or deletion of your personal data, subject to legal or contractual obligations
  • You may object to or request restriction of certain types of processing where applicable
  • You may withdraw analytics consent at any time using Cookie preferences
  • You may have additional rights depending on where you live, including rights related to portability, appeal, or lodging a complaint with a data-protection authority

10. Children's Privacy

This business website is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children through it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our website, services, or legal obligations change. We will post the revised policy here and update the date at the top.

12. Contact

For questions or requests about this policy or your personal data, contact PixelPlugins LLC in San Diego, CA at support@pixelplugins.com.
