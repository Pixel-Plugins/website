Orbit: the platform behind the websites we build

Orbit is the system PixelPlugins built to design, host, and look after our clients' websites in one place. You edit, preview, and publish. Orbit takes care of hosting, speed, security, and search setup.

Orbit
01 What Orbit is

One system for the whole life of a website

Most business websites are stitched together: a builder or CMS, a hosting account, a handful of plugins, a form service, an SEO tool, and someone who has to keep all of it updated. When one piece breaks, the website does too.

We built Orbit so that none of that is your problem. Every client gets a private account, a design made for their brand, and an address of their own. We run the platform underneath, and the people who built it are the people you call.

02 What you get

The parts of a website you should not have to think about

These come with every website on Orbit. None of them is an extra plugin to install and keep updated.

A design that is yours

Each website has its own design, built for that brand. It is not a shared template with a different logo.

Edit, preview, publish

Every change starts as a draft. Nothing your visitors see changes until someone presses Publish.

Fast pages

Pages are prepared ahead of time and delivered from a network close to the visitor. In our own measurements, most page views are answered in about a quarter of a second.

Your own domain, secured

A new website has a working address straight away. Connecting your own domain with HTTPS takes two DNS records.

Ready for search engines

The sitemap, page descriptions, and structured data are produced on every publish. Connect Google and Bing once to see your search numbers in the same place.

Enquiries that do not get lost

A form submission is saved first and emailed second, with spam checks along the way. If the email is delayed, the enquiry is still there.

07 Common questions

Orbit FAQs

Is Orbit a website builder like Wix or WordPress?

No. Orbit is not a do-it-yourself builder. It is the platform we use to build and run the websites we deliver. We design and build the site; your team edits the content and publishes when ready.

Do I need technical knowledge to use it?

No. Editing a page, adding an image, and publishing are done from an admin screen in the browser. Hosting, security certificates, and search files are handled by the platform.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. Every website gets a working address straight away, and you can connect your own domain with HTTPS by updating two DNS records. We help with that step.

What happens if I publish something by mistake?

Each publish is kept as a complete version of the website, so an earlier version can be brought back.

Can the website be in more than one language?

Yes. A website can be published in several languages, including right-to-left languages such as Arabic, and search engines are told which page belongs to which language.

Where does Orbit run?

On Google Cloud, with pages delivered through a content delivery network so they load from a location close to the visitor.

How do I get a website on Orbit?

Through a website project with us. Tell us what the website needs to do, and we will recommend whether Orbit is the right fit.
