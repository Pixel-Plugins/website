About

Your business-first technology partner

We help you make the right technology decisions—from a professional website to an advanced software platform—and keep them working as your business grows.

PixelPlugins becomes the technical side of your team. We translate business goals into practical website and product decisions, design and build the right solution, connect it to the systems you already rely on, and stay involved after launch. You keep your attention on customers and operations; we take responsibility for the technology behind them.

We are based in San Diego, CA. Alongside client work, we design and operate our own products, including DMNexa. That keeps our advice grounded in the same product, cost, reliability, and growth decisions our clients face.

How we work

We start with the business problem, not a preferred technology or a padded feature list. We will challenge unnecessary scope, explain tradeoffs in plain language, and split the roadmap into phases that create something useful before the next investment. You always know what we are solving, what comes next, and why.

What we build

  • Product strategy, technical discovery, and phased roadmaps
  • Professional company, marketing, lead-generation, and redesign websites
  • Custom SaaS platforms, B2B software, and mobile and web applications
  • AI agents, workflow automation, and human-reviewed AI systems
  • Systems integration plus custom BuildFire and Shopify development
  • Platform modernization, migrations, maintenance, and ongoing improvement
  • Compliance-ready engineering for regulated and audit-heavy operations

A partnership built to last

Some clients come to us with an idea. Others have a live product, a fragile integration, or a platform that has become expensive to change. We can meet you at any of those stages and stay through the next one—so knowledge is not lost each time the business evolves.
